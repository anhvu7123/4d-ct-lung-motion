3. Key Clinical Findings

  • Superior-Inferior (Z-axis) Dominance: Diaphragmatic excursion creates longitudinal motion that is 5 to 10 times greater than lateral (X) and anteroposterior (Y) displacements.
  • Failure of Standard Margins under Deep/Irregular Breathing: While an 8 mm margin achieves ≥95% coverage in regular breathing, it fails in deep breathing, exposing patients to severe geographical miss.
  • Recommendation: An adaptive margin expansion (10-12 mm in Z) or active motion management (DIBH/Gating per AAPM TG-76) is strictly required for deep and irregular breathers.
