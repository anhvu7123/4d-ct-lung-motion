3. Key Clinical Findings & Recommendations

  • ITV + 3 mm is effective under regular breathing, but vulnerable to baseline drift and setup uncertainty: Pass rate drops significantly when combined with realistic setup errors.
  • Alignment with AAPM TG-324 Report 91.b: An ITV setup margin of 4-5 mm is required to achieve >90% population coverage probability in the presence of 2 mm systematic and 2 mm random setup errors.
  • Anisotropic Margins Offer Superior Normal Tissue Sparing: Under regular breathing, mid-position anisotropic margins achieve 100% planning coverage while reducing PTV volume relative to ITV+3mm.
